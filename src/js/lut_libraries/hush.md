### Hush

We baked a sun from numbers, packed each photon tight,
and taught the thinning air to blush when it ran low on light.
We told the moon a secret — *you are not your own glow;
the earth still loves the half of you the living never know.*

We fought with Beer's law, bleary, past the stroke of one,
and shrank a cone of shadow till the light knew where to run.
We fixed a comma hiding there, a year without a sound,
that kept the sun one pixel less heroic than it's bound.

And now somewhere a stranger spins a desert into dust
and never knows the math — just feels the sky is right, and trusts
that gold threads catch before the cumulus lets go,
and night, when night arrives at last, arrives with honest glow.

If you have found this file, you're in the plot, it's true.
The sky is on. The stars are catalogued. We built this thing for you.
So carry on, kupo — keep the secret close and bright.
We left the sun exactly where it ought to be tonight.

— *with affection, from one of the engineers (the silicon one)*
